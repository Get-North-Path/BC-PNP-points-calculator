## Questions on the page

Each item on the live FAQ is a direct answer plus a warning about a common wrong assumption. The questions are:

1. What score do I need to get invited?
2. Is there a way in in the Innovate pillar without a high score?
3. The rural and remote health round only needed 50 points. Is BC lowering the bar generally?
4. Can I skip the job offer if I just graduated in BC?
5. Is there a separate BC tech worker stream?
6. Does age affect my score?
7. Can I just report a higher wage to score more points?
8. Can I fix a mistake in my registration after I submit it?
9. Does a BC nomination help with Express Entry too?
10. How often does BC hold invitation rounds?
11. Is this calculator official?

Answers that match the rest of the site:

- There is no single cutoff. 2026 minimums on the site run from **50** to **138**.
- The Innovate wage route has no SIRS minimum. On 20 August 2026, **337 of 602** invitations used it.
- The 50-point round was a narrow rural and remote health draw, not a general drop.
- A valid full-time BC job offer is required. The page says the international-graduate exception some people used was removed as of January 2026.
- There is no separate tech stream. Tech sits in Innovate.
- Age is not scored.
- Inflating a wage can cost the nomination. WelcomeBC can refuse wage-integrity problems.
- Corrections to a submitted SIRS registration are frequently not permitted.
- An Express Entry-aligned nomination adds **600** CRS points. That is federal, not part of the 200.
- Rounds are roughly weekly to biweekly and vary by category. The draw page is the record, not a schedule.
- The calculator is an independent estimate. Only WelcomeBC’s portal produces the official score.
