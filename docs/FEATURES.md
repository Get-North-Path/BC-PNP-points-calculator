## Calculator

**Live:** [bcpnp.getnorthpath.com](https://bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/)

| Piece | What the page does |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | “BC PNP Skills Immigration. Which category are you in, and what does it pay?” Stats: 200 points max, 3 pillars, 602 ITAs in the largest 2026 round |
| Category | Nine choices, including “not currently on a priority list” |
| Job offer | Eligible BC employer or health authority; indeterminate full-time yes/no |
| Wage | Hourly wage and an illustrative category median (default 38 for Care: Health) |
| Region | Metro Vancouver or outside Metro Vancouver |
| Experience | Months of directly related paid experience |
| Education | Secondary through doctorate, BC/Canadian credential, professional designation |
| Language | Speaking, listening, reading, writing, CLB 1–12 |
| Express Entry | Checkbox. Explains the 600 CRS nomination effect. Not part of the 200 |
| Result panel | Total, factor bars, offer gate, draw comparison, illustrative pool position, up to three next steps |
| Beyond the number | Category vs raw score, region policy, wage-integrity warning |
| FAQ teaser | Four questions, link to the full FAQ |
| Consultation | Name, email, optional phone, read-only category and score |
