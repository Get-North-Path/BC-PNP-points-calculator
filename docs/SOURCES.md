# Sources

What the live site cites, and how the methodology page grades each source. Checked **12 October 2026**.

Methodology: [bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/methodology](https://bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/methodology)

If this hub and an official page disagree, the official page wins.

## Primary sources named on the site

| Source | Used for | Status on the methodology page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Skills Immigration, WelcomeBC](https://www.welcomebc.ca/immigrate-to-b-c/skills-immigration) | Program structure, three pillars, category definitions | Primary |
| [Invitations to apply, WelcomeBC](https://www.welcomebc.ca/immigrate-to-b-c/skills-immigration/invitations-to-apply) | Draw dates, categories, invitation counts, score ranges | Primary |
| ESDC wage open data (median hourly wage by occupation and region), via [open.canada.ca](https://open.canada.ca/) | Default median-wage figures for the wage factor | Partial |
| [NOC 2021, Statistics Canada](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/subjects/standard/noc/2021/index) | TEER categories and occupation grouping | Primary |

The homepage “Sourced from” chips match those four names, plus the line “Grid in force · 04 Dec 2025 · categories restructured 23 Apr 2026.”

The draw page says WelcomeBC does not keep one clean table of every round, and that the waypoints were pulled from the invitations page.

---

## What the methodology page marks verified

- Total of **200**, and that **age is not scored**
- Category score minimums in the draw history (published round results)

## What it marks partial

- Wage factor (max 55), including the ratio bands in [SCORING-MODEL.md](SCORING-MODEL.md)
- Work experience month bands (max 40)
- Education level points and the credential and designation bonuses (max 40)
- Per-ability CLB bands (max 40)
- Region as a flat Metro Vancouver vs everywhere-else split (max 25)

The UI flag for those five factors is **EST.**

---

## Open gaps the methodology page lists

- Exact wage-to-points formula, and whether wage is compared with a BC-wide or a regional/occupational median
- Full work-experience and education tables from WelcomeBC or the SIRS user guide
- Regional-district point bands
- Priority occupation list synced to the current WelcomeBC NOC publication

---

## Out of scope, per the methodology page

- **Entrepreneur Immigration** (a different points system)
- **Health Authority** stream (no SIRS score; applied to directly through a health authority)
