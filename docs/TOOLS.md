## IELTS / CELPIP → CLB

**Live:** [bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/tools/clb-converter](https://bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/tools/clb-converter)

The page says BC’s SIRS grid scores language on the Canadian Language Benchmark scale, per ability, up to 40 points, and contrasts that with Québec’s PSTQ. It converts **IELTS General Training** or **CELPIP General**, then the reader copies CLB levels into the calculator.

The calculator’s own language cite links here. Point values for each CLB are the estimate in [SCORING-MODEL.md](SCORING-MODEL.md), not a WelcomeBC table.

The homepage language step also mentions PTE as a source of a CLB. This tool page is the IELTS and CELPIP converter.

## Priority occupation and category finder

**Live:** [bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/tools/noc-teer-finder](https://bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/tools/noc-teer-finder)

Title on the page: “Priority occupation & category finder (demo subset).”

Typing an occupation shows a typical TEER and the pillar the site usually assigns (Care, Build, or Innovate). The page says the list is a **small demo of the roughly 36 priority occupations**, not the full NOC. Anything missing should be checked on the official NOC 2021 tool and on WelcomeBC’s current priority list.

Sources named on the page: WelcomeBC’s priority occupations list and NOC 2021 V1.0 (Statistics Canada).

The five occupation guides are worked examples of that same idea. See [OCCUPATIONS.md](OCCUPATIONS.md).

## Wage vs. BC median

**Live:** [bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/tools/wage-vs-median](https://bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/tools/wage-vs-median)

Enter an offered hourly wage and a median. The page estimates the ratio and the wage-factor points (the same 55-point bands as the calculator) and checks the Innovate wage-route bar of **$55–70/hr**, which has no SIRS minimum.

It repeats the methodology warning: the median on the main form is illustrative, and the wage formula is not a published WelcomeBC table.
