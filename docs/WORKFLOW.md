# Workflow

How a person uses [bcpnp.getnorthpath.com](https://bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/). The form is one page. Category comes first because the product treats the pillar as the main differentiator.

---

## 1. Read the banner

The avis says British Columbia rebuilt Skills Immigration around Care, Build, and Innovate on **23 April 2026**, and that figures should be checked against WelcomeBC. “See what’s verified” goes to [methodology](https://bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/methodology).

---

## 2. Choose a category

Step **00** on the form: “Which category are you in?”

The control is a 2026 priority-list picker, not a free-text job title. Options:

| Value shown | Latest figure the result panel uses |
| --- | --- |
| Care: Health (priority health occupations) | Minimum 84 (range 84–108) |
| Care: Childcare (certified ECEs, NOC 42202) | Minimum 102 (range 102–115) |
| Care: Education | 68 |
| Care: Veterinary (vets and vet technologists) | 72 |
| Build: Construction trades (nine priority certified trades) | Minimum 88 (range 88–121) |
| Innovate: score route (all sectors, including tech) | Minimum 132 (range 132–138) |
| Innovate: wage route (TEER 0–3 above a wage bar; score not used) | $55/hr (range $55–70/hr) |
| Rural and remote health initiative (temporary, narrowly targeted) | 50 |
| Not currently on a priority list | No 2026 round comparison |

The cite under the control says the list changed on 23 April 2026 and will change again, and that the calculator uses the list published that date.

---

## 3. Check the job offer

Step **01**. Both Skills Immigration streams on this site require a valid offer.

- **Employer type:** eligible BC employer (Skilled Worker), or health authority (separate track, no SIRS registration)
- **Indeterminate, full-time offer?** Yes or No

Gates the result panel can show:

- Health authority: the calculator does not apply; that stream is applied to directly
- Offer is not indeterminate and full-time: the point total would not matter until that is fixed
- Occupation not on the priority list: check WelcomeBC, because lists change
- Otherwise: the offer type checks out, and category plus score still do not guarantee an invitation

---

## 4. Enter wage against a median

Step **02**. Hourly wage in CAD, compared with an illustrative BC median for the category.

The page says WelcomeBC has not confirmed whether this factor is worth 55 points or up to 100, or whether it is scored absolutely or against the median. The calculator uses the **55-point** split reported for the 4 December 2025 grid. The median is a **placeholder**. The reader is told to look up the ESDC figure for their NOC and economic region.

Default placeholders by category: Health 38, Childcare 26, Education 30, Veterinary 34, Construction 36, Innovate score route 52, Innovate wage route 55, rural health 33, not listed 35.

The wage tool does the same comparison on its own page: [tools/wage-vs-median](https://bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/tools/wage-vs-median).

---

## 5. Set the work location

Step **03**. Two choices: **Metro Vancouver** (0 region points) or **Outside Metro Vancouver** (25). The page says exact per-district bands are unconfirmed, so the calculator uses a flat 25 outside Metro. Vancouver Island and the BC Interior pages both sit in that outside-Metro group.

---

## 6. Enter experience, education, and language

- **Experience (step 04):** months of directly related paid work, in or outside Canada. Max 40. Bands are estimates.
- **Education (step 05):** highest level, plus a BC/Canadian post-secondary credential and an optional professional designation. Max 40. Sub-bands are approximated.
- **Language (step 06):** CLB per ability. The cite points at the [CLB converter](https://bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/tools/clb-converter) for raw IELTS or CELPIP bands. Per-ability points are approximated so four abilities can sum to 40.

---

## 7. Express Entry alignment

Step **07** is not part of the 200-point grid. Checking “I have an active Express Entry profile” does not change the SIRS total. The note says an Express Entry-aligned nomination adds 600 CRS points and does not apply to the base, non-EE path.

---

## 8. Read the estimate

The panel title is **Your SIRS estimate**, out of 200.

It shows:

- Pillar and category label
- Five factors, each marked **EST.** when the formula is an approximation (all five factors are marked that way)
- The offer gate from step 3
- A draw line: clears or below the latest minimum, or a wage-route message that the score is not the bar
- An illustrative pool position against the published 8,306-registration pool as of 4 August 2026 (mostly 90–129). The copy says this is not the official distribution
- Up to three “highest-impact next steps,” ordered by points still available
- A link to methodology

“Discuss my category and score” jumps to the consultation form.

---

## 9. Optional next step

- A guide, pillar, occupation, region, tool, FAQ, or the draw table
- **Request a free consultation** on the homepage. Name and email are required. Phone is optional. The read-only field is filled as `Category: … · Score: … / 200`
- **Book a walkthrough** in the header, which leaves the calculator for the parent contact page

Nothing in this flow registers the person with WelcomeBC.
