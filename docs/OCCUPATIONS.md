# Occupations

Hub: [bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/occupations](https://bcpnp.getnorthpath.com/occupations)

Title: “Priority occupations and how BC classifies them.”

These are **worked examples**, not the full priority list. The finder tool says the product ships with a demo subset of about 36 priority occupations. Confirm the current list on WelcomeBC before registering. The calculator’s category cite says the list used here is the one published with the **23 April 2026** restructure.
